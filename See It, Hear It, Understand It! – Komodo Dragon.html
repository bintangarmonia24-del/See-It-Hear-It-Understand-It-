📖 Let’s Understand Descriptive Text

What is it?

A descriptive text tells us what a person, place, animal, or thing is like.

Social Function

To describe a particular person, place, animal, or thing.

Generic Structure

1Identification – tells what we describe.

2Description – tells its parts, qualities, and characteristics.

Example

Identification: The Komodo dragon is the largest living lizard in the world.

Description: It has a large body, strong legs, sharp claws, and a long tail. It eats deer and pigs.
